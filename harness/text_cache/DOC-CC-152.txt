2. SCOPE OF WORK EXECUTED

Water Supply works for Irrigation & Waterways Dept, Govt of West Bengal completed 2025.

The work was carried out under site conditions that included hard rock encountered below the
founding level over part of the alignment. The agency organised its resources around this
without recourse to the department for additional assistance, and the programme was re-
sequenced locally to keep the critical path intact.

An extension of time was granted during execution on account of late availability of the client-
supplied cement consignment. The delay was examined by this office and held to be beyond
the agency's control; no compensation was claimed and none has been paid.

The above work was executed under the supervision of the undersigned in accordance with the
approved drawings, specifications, and the terms and conditions of the contract. The work has
been completed in all respects and has been got inspected by the Engineer-in-Chief /
Superintending Engineer.
