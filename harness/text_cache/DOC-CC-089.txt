2. SCOPE OF WORK EXECUTED

Industrial Epc works for Trishakti Power Generation Corporation completed 2023.

The work was carried out under site conditions that included expansive clay in the sub-grade
requiring additional treatment. The agency organised its resources around this without
recourse to the department for additional assistance, and the programme was re-sequenced
locally to keep the critical path intact.

An extension of time was granted during execution on account of an extended monsoon which
suspended earthwork for several weeks. The delay was examined by this office and held to be
beyond the agency's control; no compensation was claimed and none has been paid.

The above work was executed under the supervision of the undersigned in accordance with the
approved drawings, specifications, and the terms and conditions of the contract. The work has
been completed in all respects and has been got inspected by the Engineer-in-Chief /
Superintending Engineer.
