2. SCOPE OF WORK EXECUTED

Water Treatment works for Jharkhand Municipal Corporation completed 2010.

The work was carried out under site conditions that included a high water table requiring
continuous dewatering. The agency organised its resources around this without recourse to the
department for additional assistance, and the programme was re-sequenced locally to keep the
critical path intact.

An extension of time was granted during execution on account of diversion of a live utility main
discovered during excavation. The delay was examined by this office and held to be beyond
the agency's control; no compensation was claimed and none has been paid.

The above work was executed under the supervision of the undersigned in accordance with the
approved drawings, specifications, and the terms and conditions of the contract. The work has
been completed in all respects and has been got inspected by the Engineer-in-Chief /
Superintending Engineer.
