2. SCOPE OF WORK EXECUTED

Irrigation works for National Expressway Development Authority completed 2013.

The work was carried out under site conditions that included existing services running across the corridor that
had to be protected in place. The agency organised its resources around this without recourse to the department
for additional assistance, and the programme was re-sequenced locally to keep the critical path intact.

An extension of time was granted during execution on account of an extended monsoon which suspended
earthwork for several weeks. The delay was examined by this office and held to be beyond the agency's
control; no compensation was claimed and none has been paid.

The above work was executed under the supervision of the undersigned in accordance with the approved
drawings, specifications, and the terms and conditions of the contract. The work has been completed in all
respects and has been got inspected by the Engineer-in-Chief / Superintending Engineer.
