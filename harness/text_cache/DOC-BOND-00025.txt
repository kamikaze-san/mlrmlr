TERMS AND CONDITIONS

          1. Liability: Our total liability under this Bank Guarantee shall not exceed Rs. 55.46 Lakh (India Rupees Rupees 55.46 Lakh
             Only).

          2. Validity: This Guarantee shall remain valid from 2019-10-08 until 2023-07-27 (the "Expiry Date") unless extended by
             mutual consent in writing.

          3. Claim Period: We shall be liable to pay under this guarantee only if a written claim or demand is served upon us on or
             before the Expiry Date.

          4. Enforceability: Any claim arising under this guarantee shall be enforceable at our branch office at Mumbai or at any
             scheduled bank branch in India as nominated by the claimant.

          5. No Defence: Our obligation to pay is absolute and unconditional. We shall have no right of set-off, counterclaim, or
             defence arising from any dispute between the Contractor and the Employer.

          6. Modification: This guarantee shall not be affected by any change in the constitution, ownership, or control of the
             Contractor or the Employer.

          7. Governing Law: This guarantee is governed by the laws of India and is subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in
             Mumbai.
