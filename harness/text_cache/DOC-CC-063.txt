2. SCOPE OF WORK EXECUTED

Roads Highways works for Public Health Engineering Dept, Odisha completed 2020.

The work was carried out under site conditions that included existing services running across the
corridor that had to be protected in place. The agency organised its resources around this without
recourse to the department for additional assistance, and the programme was re-sequenced locally to
keep the critical path intact.

An extension of time was granted during execution on account of late availability of the client-supplied
cement consignment. The delay was examined by this office and held to be beyond the agency's
control; no compensation was claimed and none has been paid.

The above work was executed under the supervision of the undersigned in accordance with the
approved drawings, specifications, and the terms and conditions of the contract. The work has been
completed in all respects and has been got inspected by the Engineer-in-Chief / Superintending
Engineer.
