2. SCOPE OF WORK EXECUTED

Roads Maintenance works for Jharkhand Municipal Corporation completed 2017.

The work was carried out under site conditions that included restricted access requiring night-
time material movement. The agency organised its resources around this without recourse to
the department for additional assistance, and the programme was re-sequenced locally to keep
the critical path intact.

An extension of time was granted during execution on account of restricted working hours
imposed near the adjoining school. The delay was examined by this office and held to be
beyond the agency's control; no compensation was claimed and none has been paid.

The above work was executed under the supervision of the undersigned in accordance with the
approved drawings, specifications, and the terms and conditions of the contract. The work has
been completed in all respects and has been got inspected by the Engineer-in-Chief /
Superintending Engineer.
