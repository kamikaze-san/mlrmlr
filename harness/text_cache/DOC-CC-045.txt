2. SCOPE OF WORK EXECUTED

Sewerage Drainage works for Public Health Engineering Dept, Odisha completed 2020.

The work was carried out under site conditions that included restricted access requiring night-time
material movement. The agency organised its resources around this without recourse to the
department for additional assistance, and the programme was re-sequenced locally to keep the critical
path intact.

An extension of time was granted during execution on account of an extended monsoon which
suspended earthwork for several weeks. The delay was examined by this office and held to be beyond
the agency's control; no compensation was claimed and none has been paid.

The above work was executed under the supervision of the undersigned in accordance with the
approved drawings, specifications, and the terms and conditions of the contract. The work has been
completed in all respects and has been got inspected by the Engineer-in-Chief / Superintending
Engineer.
