E. NOTES TO ACCOUNTS

Note 1 -- Basis of Preparation: These financial statements have been prepared in
accordance with Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS) as notified under the
Companies Act, 2013. The financial statements have been prepared on a historical
cost basis except for certain financial instruments measured at fair value.

Note 2 -- Revenue Recognition: Revenue from construction contracts is recognised
over time using the input method (costs incurred to date relative to total
estimated costs) in accordance with Ind AS 115 -- Revenue from Contracts with
Customers.

Note 3 -- Going Concern: Based on the Company's financial position, including the
order book of approximately 603 Cr and positive net worth, the management is
satisfied that the Company is a going concern and there is no material
uncertainty.

Note 4 -- Bank Solvency: The Company maintains satisfactory banking
relationships. Bank solvency certificate status: Sufficient.

F. AUDITOR'S REPORT (EXTRACT)

statements.

Opinion: In our opinion and to the best of our information and according to the
explanations given to us, the aforesaid financial statements give a true and
fair view in conformity with Ind AS of the state of affairs of the Company as at
31st March 2024, and of its profit and cash flows for the year then ended.
