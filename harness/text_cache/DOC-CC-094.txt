2. SCOPE OF WORK EXECUTED

Sewerage Drainage works for Subarnarekha Valley Corporation completed 2014.

The work was carried out under site conditions that included restricted access requiring night-time material
movement. The agency organised its resources around this without recourse to the department for additional
assistance, and the programme was re-sequenced locally to keep the critical path intact.

An extension of time was granted during execution on account of a revision to the approved drawings issued
after mobilisation. The delay was examined by this office and held to be beyond the agency's control; no
compensation was claimed and none has been paid.

The above work was executed under the supervision of the undersigned in accordance with the approved
drawings, specifications, and the terms and conditions of the contract. The work has been completed in all
respects and has been got inspected by the Engineer-in-Chief / Superintending Engineer.
