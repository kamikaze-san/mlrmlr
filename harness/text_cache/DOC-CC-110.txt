2. SCOPE OF WORK EXECUTED

Expressways works for Trishakti Power Generation Corporation completed 2015.

The work was carried out under site conditions that included hard rock encountered below the
founding level over part of the alignment. The agency organised its resources around this without
recourse to the department for additional assistance, and the programme was re-sequenced locally to
keep the critical path intact.

An extension of time was granted during execution on account of delayed handover of the site
frontage by the local authority. The delay was examined by this office and held to be beyond the
agency's control; no compensation was claimed and none has been paid.

The above work was executed under the supervision of the undersigned in accordance with the
approved drawings, specifications, and the terms and conditions of the contract. The work has been
completed in all respects and has been got inspected by the Engineer-in-Chief / Superintending
Engineer.
