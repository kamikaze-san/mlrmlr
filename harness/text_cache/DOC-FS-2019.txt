E. Notes to Accounts

Note 1 -- Basis of Preparation: These financial statements have been prepared in accordance with Indian
Accounting Standards (Ind AS) as notified under the Companies Act, 2013. The financial statements have
been prepared on a historical cost basis except for certain financial instruments measured at fair value.

Note 2 -- Revenue Recognition: Revenue from construction contracts is recognised over time using the
input method (costs incurred to date relative to total estimated costs) in accordance with Ind AS 115 --
Revenue from Contracts with Customers.

Note 3 -- Going Concern: Based on the Company's financial position, including the order book of
approximately 120 Cr and positive net worth, the management is satisfied that the Company is a going
concern and there is no material uncertainty.

Note 4 -- Bank Solvency: The Company maintains satisfactory banking relationships. Bank solvency
certificate status: Sufficient.

F. Auditor's Report (Extract)

We have audited the accompanying financial statements of National Infrastructure Corp. Ltd. which
comprise the Balance Sheet as at 31st March 2020, the Statement of Profit and Loss for the year then ended,
and notes to the financial statements.

Opinion: In our opinion and to the best of our information and according to the explanations given to us,
the aforesaid financial statements give a true and fair view in conformity with Ind AS of the state of affairs
of the Company as at 31st March 2020, and of its profit and cash flows for the year then ended.
