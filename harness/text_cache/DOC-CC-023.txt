2. SCOPE OF WORK EXECUTED

Water Treatment works for Mahanadi Steel Corporation completed 2022.

The work was carried out under site conditions that included expansive clay in
the sub-grade requiring additional treatment. The agency organised its resources
around this without recourse to the department for additional assistance, and
the programme was re-sequenced locally to keep the critical path intact.

An extension of time was granted during execution on account of delayed handover
of the site frontage by the local authority. The delay was examined by this
office and held to be beyond the agency's control; no compensation was claimed
and none has been paid.

The above work was executed under the supervision of the undersigned in
accordance with the approved drawings, specifications, and the terms and
conditions of the contract. The work has been completed in all respects and has
been got inspected by the Engineer-in-Chief / Superintending Engineer.
