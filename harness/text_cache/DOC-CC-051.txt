2. SCOPE OF WORK EXECUTED

Water Treatment works for Lakshya Engineering & Construction completed 2022.

The work was carried out under site conditions that included hard rock
encountered below the founding level over part of the alignment. The agency
organised its resources around this without recourse to the department for
additional assistance, and the programme was re-sequenced locally to keep the
critical path intact.

An extension of time was granted during execution on account of an extended
monsoon which suspended earthwork for several weeks. The delay was examined by
this office and held to be beyond the agency's control; no compensation was
claimed and none has been paid.

The above work was executed under the supervision of the undersigned in
accordance with the approved drawings, specifications, and the terms and
conditions of the contract. The work has been completed in all respects and has
been got inspected by the Engineer-in-Chief / Superintending Engineer.
