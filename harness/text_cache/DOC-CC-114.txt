2. SCOPE OF WORK EXECUTED

Tunnels works for Jal Nigam, Gujarat completed 2010.

The work was carried out under site conditions that included a high water table
requiring continuous dewatering. The agency organised its resources around this
without recourse to the department for additional assistance, and the programme
was re-sequenced locally to keep the critical path intact.

An extension of time was granted during execution on account of restricted
working hours imposed near the adjoining school. The delay was examined by this
office and held to be beyond the agency's control; no compensation was claimed
and none has been paid.

The above work was executed under the supervision of the undersigned in
accordance with the approved drawings, specifications, and the terms and
conditions of the contract. The work has been completed in all respects and has
been got inspected by the Engineer-in-Chief / Superintending Engineer.
